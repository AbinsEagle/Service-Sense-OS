Power rails run across the top. The battery feeds the board's 5V pin, and the board's own AMS1117 makes the 3V3 rail that powers the ESP32, ADS1115, DS18B20, TDS, LEDs and buttons. The MT3608 makes 5V only for the ZMPT101B and the pressure sensor. Dots mark joints; crossings without a dot are not connected. Scroll sideways on mobile.
Battery on the 5V pin. Fresh cells give up to ~6.4V. The onboard AMS1117 is rated to 15V, so this is the PRD topology, but it's above the pin's 5V label. To stay within it, feed the 5V pin from the MT3608 output instead. Either way, switch the battery off while USB is plugged in.
ZMPT101B divider (added, not in the PRD). On a 5V supply its output idles at ~2.5V and can swing above 3.6V, which is past the ADS1115's absolute maximum when the ADC runs on 3.3V. A 10k/15k divider (like the pressure sensor's) keeps it under 3V.
Sleep current. The dev board's USB-serial chip, power LED and regulator draw milliamps in deep sleep, so the <10 µA target can only be measured on the custom PCB.
